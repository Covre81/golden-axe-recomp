Golden Axe Recomp - test build
==============================

Unofficial native PC port of Golden Axe (Sega Genesis / Mega Drive).
Not affiliated with Sega.

YOU MUST SUPPLY YOUR OWN ROM. None is included.
Supported ROM: Golden Axe (World) (Rev A), 524,288 bytes, CRC32 665D7DF9.

How to run
----------
1. Extract the whole zip to a folder (keep assets/ and SDL2.dll next to the exe).
2. Run GoldenAxeRecomp.exe.
3. In the launcher, select your ROM file. The choice is remembered.

Controls can be remapped in the launcher.
F7 = save-state browser, F8 = rewind browser.

Reporting bugs
--------------
https://github.com/Covre81/golden-axe-recomp/issues
Please include: the stage and what you were doing, what happened versus the
original game, a save state from just before it (native_save_N.bin, made with
F7) or a screenshot/video, your OS, and keyboard or controller. Also attach
build-info.json from this folder.
